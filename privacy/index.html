Privacy Policy

Untag collects no data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Untag is built to check your surroundings without watching you. We don't collect any personal data. Everything Untag does happens on your iPhone, and no data about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

Bluetooth

Untag listens for Bluetooth signals near you to recognize trackers. To confirm a possible AirTag, it may briefly connect to that nearby device and ask which services it offers. Those signals are analyzed on your device and are not uploaded or shared.

Location (Trip mode only)

When you turn on Trip mode, Untag uses your location to tell places apart, so it can notice a tag that comes with you to a place and stays near you there. This log is kept in memory on your iPhone, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you stop Trip mode.

Camera (Lens check only)

Lens check uses the camera and flashlight to look for lens reflections. Frames are analyzed in memory and are never saved or sent.

Tags you mark as yours

When you tap "It's mine" on a tracker, Untag keeps a short code for that tracker on your iPhone so it doesn't list it again (for 24 hours, or until you forget it in Settings for tags that keep the same signal). It's never sent anywhere.

Local network

Untag looks for cameras on the Wi-Fi you're connected to. It listens for devices that announce themselves and sends small discovery requests to devices on that network to see whether they stream video. It never logs in to them or views their video, and nothing is sent to the internet.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

No tracking

Untag has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. We don't track you across apps or websites.

Children

Untag is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: untag.app@hotmail.com.
