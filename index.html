Untag Support

Checks a room, a car or a trip for hidden trackers and cameras, right on your iPhone.

Need help? Email untag.app@hotmail.com. We answer in English, usually within 2 business days.

What Untag checks

  • Bluetooth trackers nearby: Tile, Galaxy SmartTag and Find Hub tags by their public signals, and AirTag-style items when Untag can confirm them up close.
  • Cameras on the Wi-Fi: devices that announce a video stream on the network you're connected to.
  • Lens check: your flashlight blinks and Untag marks tiny, isolated points that blink back, like the reflection of a camera lens.
  • Trip mode: a private log of trackers around you while you move; Untag warns you if the same one shows up at 3 places with you.

Common questions

Why do I see "Apple device or AirTag"?
iOS hides from apps the detail that tells an AirTag apart from an iPhone, a Mac or AirPods. Untag only counts an AirTag after checking it up close. Your iPhone also warns you about unknown AirTags on its own: keep those alerts on.

What can't Untag see?
No app can promise a room is clean. Cameras that record to a memory card or use their own network don't show up on the Wi-Fi. Trackers that don't use Bluetooth, like cellular GPS trackers, can't be seen by any iPhone app. Lens check works best in the dark, within about 3 m, on lenses in plain sight; shiny surfaces like keyboards and metal have too many reflections to tell.

I found something that isn't mine. What now?
Don't remove it if you feel unsafe. Apple's Personal Safety guide explains next steps, and you can contact local law enforcement.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Untag Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
