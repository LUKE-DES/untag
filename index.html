What Untag checks

  • Bluetooth trackers nearby: Tile, Galaxy SmartTag, Chipolo, Pebblebee and Find Hub tags by their public signals, and AirTags when Untag can check them up close. Tags whose own signal says they're with their owner are shown apart.
  • Cameras on the Wi-Fi: devices on the network you're connected to that announce themselves as a camera or answer like one. When the network keeps devices apart, Untag says it can't see.
  • Trip mode: a private log of Tile, SmartTag, Chipolo, Pebblebee and Find Hub tags while you move. Untag warns you when one travels with you to a place and is still near you 10 minutes later. Your iPhone covers AirTags with its own alerts.

Your first 3 room checks are free. Untag Pro adds unlimited room checks, the finder and Trip mode.

Common questions

Why does Untag say "Not sure about AirTags"?
iOS hides from apps the detail that tells an AirTag apart from an iPhone, a Mac or AirPods. Untag connects to the Apple devices close to you for a moment to check. When one doesn't answer, Untag says it's not sure instead of guessing. Your iPhone also warns you about unknown AirTags that move with you: keep Tracking Notifications on.

What can't Untag see?
No app can promise a room is clean. Cameras that record to a memory card or use their own network don't show up on the Wi-Fi. Trackers that don't use Bluetooth, like cellular GPS trackers, can't be seen by any iPhone app.

Does Trip mode know if I'm being followed?
It can't know for sure, and it never says so. It tells you what it heard: when the same tag traveled with you to a place and was still near you 10 minutes later, it warns that the tag is likely moving with you, and shows where and when it was heard.

Can Untag tell whose tracker it is?
No. Untag shows what it heard and how close it is, so you can find it. It can't see who owns a tracker.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Untag Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
